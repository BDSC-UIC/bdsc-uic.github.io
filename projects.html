Agentic RAG and Deep Research: We build and evaluate retrieval-augmented reasoning systems that plan, search, and synthesize evidence into long-form reports, with attention to open-domain evaluation and safety guardrails. Towards Agentic RAG with Deep Reasoning: A Survey of RAG-Reasoning Systems in LLMs (EMNLP 2025) · Awesome-RAG-Reasoning · Awesome-Deep-Research

Agentic Reinforcement Learning and LLM Post-Training: We develop post-training methods that scale reasoning, including curriculum and bandit-driven problem curation, many-shot in-context learning with self-generated demonstrations, and co-evolutionary verification of agent skills. Actor-Curator: Co-adaptive Problem Curation via Policy-Improvement Bandits (COLM 2026)

Generative Models for Structured Data and Time Series: Tabular data synthesis and watermarking, diffusion language models, missing-data imputation, and latent-state time series forecasting. TabNAT: A Continuous-Discrete Joint Generative Framework for Tabular Data (ICML 2025)

Graph Mining and Outlier Detection: Graph anomaly and fraud detection, graph self-training, and scalable temporal graph transformers, together with the lab's open-source toolkit. PyGOD: A Python Library for Graph Outlier Detection

Human-Agent Collaboration and Interactive Systems: Fully autonomous agents remain unreliable for many real tasks. We study systems where humans and agents share control, exchange feedback, and divide work, including interactive research-code development. LLM-Based Human-Agent Collaboration and Interaction Systems: A Survey (ACL 2026) · Awesome-Human-Agent-Collaboration

LLM Agents and Agent Memory: We study how agents accumulate, organize, and reuse experience so they can act coherently over long horizons and improve themselves over time. Work spans memory architectures, self-evolving skills, and personalized agents. A Survey of Agent Memory in the Second Half: Towards Self-Evolving and Long-Horizon Agents (TMLR 2026, Survey Certification) · Awesome-Agent-Memory

Recommender Systems and Personalization: A long-standing lab strength, now driven by LLM agents and graph-language alignment: agents that call recommendation tools, prompt refinement for reranking, and cold-start and session-based recommendation. AgentDR: Dynamic Recommendation with Implicit Item-Item Relations via LLM-based Agents (WWW 2026)

Trustworthy LLMs: Hallucination, Safety and Evaluation: We analyze why models hallucinate when reasoning over structured knowledge, build benchmarks and judges for agent behavior, and study watermarking, unlearning, and multimodal safety. Why LLMs Hallucinate on Structured Knowledge: A Mechanistic Analysis (ACL 2026) · MultiFileTest (ACL 2026)

Uncertainty Quantification and Conformal Prediction: Toward models that know when they should not answer. We develop conformal methods for graphs and stream networks, data selection under distribution shift, and deployment audits for high-stakes triage. Topology-Aware Conformal Prediction for Stream Networks (NeurIPS 2025)

Funded Projects: Exploiting the Massive User Generated Utterances for Intent Mining under Scarce Annotations (NSF 1909323) · Learning Dynamic and Robust Defenses Against Co-Adaptive Spammers (NSF 1930941)
